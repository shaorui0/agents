c0001 requirements-and-method

What changed

  • docs/METHOD.md: the model. Context as scarce RAM, four primitives, phase-lock state machine, four-gate flow, knowledge compounding, a step table and a failure-mode table. Every rule says enforced or advised.
  • docs/REQUIREMENTS.md: R1-R9, deliverables D1-D5, data boundary.
  • .claude/skills/agents-change-loop/SKILL.md, LICENSE (MIT).
  • tools/check_rules.py: leak scan, required files, doc shape, legacy files untouched, commit-subject format.

Why

The repo published structure but not reasoning, and nothing in it could be run. Without a model, “safe” is a feeling. Writing which rules are enforced (exit codes) versus advised (prompts) is what makes the next two changes testable.

Result

  • make test green (4 tests), check_rules: OK, 2 mermaid diagrams, openspec validate valid.
  • Legacy scan found 36 hit lines in 11 pre-existing files (real-looking cluster aliases, a ticket id, an internal-name path). They were already public. They were listed, not edited. This is the strongest argument for the leak scan being a gate rather than a habit.
